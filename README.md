## About France Sire

**France Sire** is a **Comedy Club** in **Charlotte** — Club showcasing local and touring comics weekly.

We publish hours, offerings and contact paths clearly so customers know what to expect before they visit or book.

## What drives us

France Sire is a comedy club built around real neighbors in Charlotte. We'd rather do fewer things well than overwhelm you with noise.

- Curated offerings over endless catalogs
- Human replies instead of auto-responses
- Seasonal updates that actually change
- A front door that's easy to find

## Looking ahead

Expand what France Sire does best while keeping the same welcome — evolving with entertainment trends without losing our local voice.
