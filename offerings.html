    Where are you?

    100 Tryon St, Charlotte, NC. See the map on Visit.

    How do I reach support?

    Use the Contact form or email support@francesire.com. We reply during open hours.

    How do I book?

    Send a message on Contact, email bookings@francesire.com, or call +1 (704) 555-0137.

    Are prices current?

    Listed prices are current and may shift with seasons — we update this site when they do.

    Do you take groups?

    Yes for many bookings — tell us the size and date and we'll confirm what France Sire can host.

    What should I bring?

    Just yourself (and any notes on allergies, sizes, or preferences). We'll handle the rest.

    Where do I park?

    See the Visit page for address and map links around Charlotte.

    Do you update shows often?

    Yes — seasonal shifts are posted here first.

    Can I book for someone else?

    Yes — include their name and preferences in the Contact form or email support@francesire.com.
